\section{Adequacy}
\label{sec:adequacy}

Each of our five correctness results speaks of a different semantics.
As due diligence on our mechanized definitions, we prove the graph
model adequate with respect to the clocked interpreters, the CEK
machines, and the reduction semantics, and we prove the Scott-domain
semantics adequate with respect to the clocked interpreters. Each
proof adapts a proof from the literature
\citep{Plotkin:1977oc,Plotkin:1985aa,Winskel:1993uq,Siek:2017ab}.
Here is the statement of adequacy of the graph model with respect to
the interpreter.

\begin{proposition}[Adequacy]\label{prop:adequacy}
Let $M$ be a closed term of \fpc. For every constant $c$, $c \in
\den{M}$ iff the interpreter of \fpc\ terminates on $M$ returning $c$,
and $\den{M}$ is empty iff the interpreter diverges on $M$, that is,
times out at every clock.
\end{proposition}

The graph-model is also adequate with respect to the CEK machines of
\fpc\ and \instr\ and with the reduction semantics of \fpc, whose
correctness proof targets the CEK machine of \instr. The adequacy
results therefore cover every semantics in our comparison and make the
five correctness results equivalent.
