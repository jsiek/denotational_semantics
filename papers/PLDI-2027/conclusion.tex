\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We proved a ten-pass compiler correct, from a call-by-value
$\lambda$-calculus with recursive types down to a typed assembly
language, with a graph-model semantics for every language, and
mechanized the proof in Agda (RQ1). Nine passes are proved by short
equational calculations (RQ2). The exception, \pass{delay}, needs the
approximant logical relation for reflection (RQ3). Among three
operational proofs of the same compiler, the denotational proof ranks
second in size (RQ4), and a second denotational proof, with Scott
domains, is 1.4 times as large (RQ5). In future work we plan to report on a dynamically
typed variant of the compiler and to repeat the comparison in Rocq or
Lean.
