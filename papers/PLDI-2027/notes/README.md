# Notes for the related-work section

Research notes, not paper text, compiled on 2026-10-14 for the related-work and history parts of the paper, about the logical relation used for the reflection direction of the delay pass (the finite target list against an arbitrary source set; `agda/proof/Correctness/{Graph,GraphErr}/DelayRel.agda`).

- `related-work.md`: all the literature notes in one file, with BibTeX. §1 lists the novelty claims (from the abstract, the outline and `contributions.tex`) with verdicts and safe wording; §2 is a set of tables that categorize every paper against those claims; §3 indexes the PDFs in `related-denot/` (repository root, not in git). Part I is the novelty search: mechanized compiler proofs with denotational semantics, graph and filter models, and adequacy for assembly languages. Part II is the survey of closure conversion with denotational semantics and mechanized proofs. Part III covers logical relations: relations for adequacy (Part A, a denotation against syntax or an operational semantics) and relations between two denotational semantics (Part B, where the approximant relation belongs), with a name check for "finitary" and "approximant". Claims are marked by how closely they were checked (**[V]**, **[A]**, **[U]** and so on; the legend is at the top of the file).
- `delay-relation-history.md`: how the delay relation evolved in this repository from 2019 to 2026, with commits, issues and PRs, the quoted definitions and outcomes. Each attempt is labelled A (adequacy) or B (between two denotations). It ends with the provenance of the current relation.
- `history-outline.md`: an outline for presenting the history in the paper, as a tour of the relation shapes that were tried and why each fails, leading to the insight. It also lists the double-blind and tone constraints.
- `paper-outline.md`: the outline of the paper, around its main claim (a mechanized graph-model proof of the ISWIMμ compiler) and four research questions.
- `vlisp-clinger-wand.md`: VLISP and Clinger 1984 (paper-only denotational compiler proofs) and Wand 1991 (a paper proof in an equational λ-theory, not a denotational semantics): none performs a closure-conversion pass through to assembly, and none is mechanized.
- `iswim-mu-design.md`: the design of the ISWIMμ experiment (a typed ISWIM with iso-recursive types, no recursion construct and no errors, against the typed pipeline), v2 as approved, with notes marked **[P0]** where the pilot (`agda/Mu/`) corrected or settled it.
- `mixed-semantics.md`: the experiment of mixing semantics on ISWIMμ: the cheaper of the graph-model and interpreter proofs per pass, joined by bridges (adequacy of a language's graph semantics with respect to its interpreter). It has the design and estimates, and the results of split (a), built in `agda/Mu/proof/Mixed/`. Mixing does not pay (1839 against the interpreter column's 1653). The bridge is a cheaper ISWIMμ adequacy (395 against 464 w.r.t. the CEK machine).
