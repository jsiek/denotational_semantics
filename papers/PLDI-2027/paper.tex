\section*{Data-Availability Statement}

The artifact is our Agda development: the five correctness proofs of the
compiler, the adequacy of the graph-model semantics, the shared
definitions of the languages and passes, and the script that computes
the proof sizes of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}. It checks with Agda
2.8 and its standard library 2.4 in safe mode, with no postulates, in
about three minutes on a desktop workstation (Intel Core Ultra 9 285K,
64\,GB). The Scott-domain proof also needs a pinned, lightly patched version of TypeTopology, likewise checked in safe mode, and about three more minutes; its theorems take propositional truncation, function extensionality, and propositional extensionality as hypotheses. The README of the artifact
lists the commands that check each development and regenerate
Table~\ref{tab:loc}, the pinned TypeTopology commit and patch, and the
files that hold the theorem statements. An anonymized version is provided as
supplementary material, and we will submit it for Artifact Evaluation.
