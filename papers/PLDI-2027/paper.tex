\section*{Data-Availability Statement}

The artifact is our Agda development: the five correctness proofs of the
compiler, the adequacy of the graph-model semantics, the shared
definitions of the languages and passes, and the script that computes
the proof sizes of Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}. It checks with Agda
2.8 and its standard library 2.4 in safe mode, with no postulates, in
about two minutes from scratch on a desktop workstation (Intel Core Ultra 9 285K,
64\,GB). The Scott-domain proof also needs the modules of TypeTopology that it imports, which the artifact includes, lightly patched, from a pinned commit. With them, the Scott-domain proof checks in safe mode in about two and a half more minutes, and its theorems take propositional truncation, function extensionality, and propositional extensionality as hypotheses. A Dockerfile builds and checks everything. The README of the artifact
lists the commands that check each development and regenerate
Tables~\ref{tab:loc} and~\ref{tab:infra}, and it maps each claim of the paper
to the file that states it. An anonymized version is provided as
supplementary material, and we will submit it for Artifact Evaluation.
