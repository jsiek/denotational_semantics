\section*{Data-Availability Statement}

The artifact is our Agda development that mechanizes the results of
this paper: the four proofs of correctness of the compiler (graph
model, abstract machines, clocked interpreters, and reduction
semantics), the adequacy of the graph-model semantics, the approximant
logical relation, and the shared definitions of the languages and
passes, together with the script that computes the proof sizes in
Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}. The development checks with Agda 2.8 and
its standard library 2.4 in safe mode, with no postulates, in about
three minutes on a desktop workstation (an Intel Core Ultra 9 285K
with 64\,GB of memory), and the theorem statements are collected in a
few files for inspection. An anonymized version is provided as
supplementary material, and we will submit the artifact for Artifact
Evaluation.
